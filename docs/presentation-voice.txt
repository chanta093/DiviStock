Dividend operations still rely on closed spreadsheets, multiple intermediaries, and long settlement windows. Holders cannot independently verify their allocation or see whether the distribution pool is fully funded.

DiviStock is a Solana distribution rail for tokenized equity. An issuer takes a shareholder snapshot, commits it as a Merkle root, funds a USDC vault, and lets each eligible holder claim their exact allocation.

Here is one SOLX distribution on Devnet. The pool is twenty-five thousand USDC. The snapshot contains one million eligible shares, and claim progress is visible to everyone. Each holder sees only the entitlement committed to their wallet.

This wallet owns five thousand SOLX shares. At two point five cents per share, its allocation is one hundred and twenty-five USDC. The Merkle proof binds this wallet and this exact amount to the published snapshot root.

When the holder submits a claim, the program recomputes the leaf, verifies the proof, transfers USDC from the program-controlled vault, and creates a claim receipt. The receipt is a program-derived address unique to this distribution and holder.

After the claim, the available allocation becomes zero and the aggregate claimed amount rises by one hundred and twenty-five USDC. A second claim cannot succeed because the receipt already exists. This rule is enforced by the program, not a web server.

DiviStock makes the path from funded distribution to received dividend clear and inspectable. Our next steps are issuer governance, audited USDC vaults, and multiple distributions. Thank you.
